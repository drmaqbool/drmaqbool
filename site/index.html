About

Dr Mohd Maqbool Sohil is an interventional cardiologist in India specialising in complex PCI, rotablation (rotational atherectomy) and IVUS-guided angioplasty. He has 12 years of experience, about 10,000 cardiovascular interventions (including ~9,000 coronary angioplasties and ~1,000 non-coronary cardiovascular interventions) and ~20,000 angiographies.

He trained in Internal Medicine at PGIMER Chandigarh and in Cardiology at SKIMS Srinagar, and is a Fellow of the Society for Cardiovascular Angiography and Interventions (FSCAI, 2021). He specialises in complex high-risk indicated PCI (CHIP), rotational atherectomy, left main and bifurcation stenting, chronic total occlusions and intravascular imaging-guided PCI, using a radial-first approach, and is a proctor for Boston Scientific in rotational atherectomy.

His career spans major cardiac centres across India: 7.5 years as Senior Consultant in Interventional Cardiology at Narayana Health, Ahmedabad (one of India’s largest networks of cardiac centres), and a leadership role at Metro Heart Institute, Faridabad (Delhi NCR). He is currently Director of Interventional Cardiology at Paras Hospitals, Srinagar, where he was the first interventional cardiologist in Jammu & Kashmir to establish IVUS and rotational atherectomy in the private sector. He is the author of peer-reviewed publications on rotablation-assisted left main PCI, calcified coronary disease, intracoronary stem-cell therapy and cardiac pacing.

Clinical expertise

Procedural volume

  • About 10,000 cardiovascular interventions, including ~9,000 coronary angioplasties and ~1,000 non-coronary cardiovascular interventions; ~20,000 angiographies over 12 years.
  • Currently about 200 procedures per month, including approximately 150 coronary angiographies and 60–100 angioplasties.
  • Approximately 800 cardiac rhythm devices implanted: single- and dual-chamber pacemakers, CRT-P, CRT-D and ICDs.

Complex coronary intervention

  • Complex high-risk indicated PCI (CHIP) performed routinely, including interventions in cardiogenic shock.
  • Rotational atherectomy: high-volume operator (70–100 procedures per year) and proctor for Boston Scientific.
  • Bifurcation lesions including left main bifurcations; calcified lesions, including calcified bifurcations with depressed LV systolic function; diffuse disease.
  • Coronary chronic total occlusion (CTO) interventions performed routinely.
  • Primary PCI: high-volume operator (approx. 12–15 per month), other emergency coronary interventions and IABP insertion.

Intravascular imaging, physiology & access

  • IVUS guidance in 80–90% of all coronary and peripheral interventions.
  • Radial-first operator: about 90% of coronary angiographies and interventions via the radial artery; also uses distal (dorsal) radial access.

Peripheral & structural intervention

  • Peripheral angiography and angioplasty, including complex lesions and chronic total occlusions; carotid and aortic interventions.
  • Structural heart interventions: PDA, ASD and VSD device closure.
