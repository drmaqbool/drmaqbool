## Single centre experience of rotablation-assisted left main percutaneous coronary intervention

Sohil MM, Masood I, Goyal D, Rashid H. Single centre experience of rotablation-assisted left main percutaneous coronary intervention. *American Heart Journal Plus: Cardiology Research and Practice* 2026;61:100678. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ahjo.2025.100678](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ahjo.2025.100678)

Open access, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. 17 slides.

Key results: 45 patients; SYNTAX score 40.1 ± 8.2; angiographic success 100%; procedural success 97.8%; two-year MACE-free survival 93.3%.
