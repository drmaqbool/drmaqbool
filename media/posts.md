### World Heart Day 2026: “Don’t Miss a Beat”

**Posted:** 30 September 2026 · [Original Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/photo.php?fbid=10246063201864252)  
Facebook labels this post as AI content. Image: [science/2026-09-30-world-heart-day-2026-dont-miss-a-beat.jpg](science/2026-09-30-world-heart-day-2026-dont-miss-a-beat.jpg)

> ❤️ World Heart Day 2026: “Don’t Miss a Beat” 🥁
> 🚨 The Message: Cardiovascular Disease Doesn't Always Start with an Emergency
> Every year on September 29th, World Heart Day brings the global medical community together. Under the World Heart Federation's "Don't Miss a Beat" campaign, our focus this year is clear: cardiovascular disease frequently hides in plain sight.
> As interventional cardiologists, we often meet patients in the Cath Lab during an acute coronary event. But the ultimate goal of modern cardiology is early detection, precision intervention, and prevention.
> Unexplained exertional fatigue, mild shortness of breath, silent hypertension, or transient chest discomfort are not things to ignore—they are warning beats.
> 🔬 The Latest 2026 Evidence-Based Advances in Cath Lab Precision
> Interventional cardiology has shifted from "open the vessel" to precision vascular restoration. Here is what current 2026 clinical evidence highlights:
>  * 🩺 Intravascular Imaging Guidance (IVUS & OCT): Large-scale multi-center network meta-analyses show that IVUS- and OCT-guided PCI reduce Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events (MACE) by up to 31% compared to traditional angiography alone. Precision sizing, plaque characterization, and stent optimization are now the gold standard in complex interventions.
>  * ⚡ Intravascular Lithotripsy (IVL) & Drug-Coated Balloons (DCB): Sonic waves break severe medial calcium safely without tissue trauma, while drug-coated balloons offer "leave-nothing-behind" strategies for in-stent restenosis and small-vessel disease.
>  * 🤖 AI-CT Angiography & Non-Invasive FFR-CT: Advanced imaging identifies pericoronary adipose tissue (PCAT) inflammation and vulnerable plaque years before a lesion causes hemodynamic ischemia.
> 📋 4 Steps Every Patient Must Take Today
> | Priority Area | Target / Action | Why It Matters |
> |---|---|---|
> | 🩸 Know Your Numbers | Blood Pressure < 120/80 mmHg; LDL-C tailored to risk profile. | High BP is silent—it causes vascular wall stress without symptoms. |
> | 🏃 Active Movement | Minimum 150 mins/week moderate physical activity. | Keeps endothelium flexible and improves myocardial perfusion. |
> | 🚭 Tobacco Cessation | Zero exposure to active or second-hand smoke. | Smoking directly triggers endothelial damage and platelet hyperreactivity. |
> | 🩺 Routine Screening | Check Lp(a), High-Sensitivity CRP, and Lipid Panels early. | Lipoprotein(a) is genetic and an independent risk factor for early atherosclerosis. |
> 💬 A Message From Your Cath Lab Team
> > "Our catheters and stents can restore blood flow in minutes, but early prevention and timely medical evaluation save decades of life."
> > 
> Don't wait for a dramatic symptom. Get screened, know your risk factors, and listen to what your heart is telling you.
> #WorldHeartDay2026 #DontMissABeat #InterventionalCardiology #HeartHealth #PCI #CathLab #UseHeartForAction

### Multi-vessel PCI: RCA CTO and LAD/diagonal bifurcation

**Posted:** 29 November 2025 · [Original Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/mohd.m.sohil/videos/1849203309012022/)  
Video: [procedures/2025-11-29-pci-rca-cto-and-lad-diagonal-bifurcation.mp4](procedures/2025-11-29-pci-rca-cto-and-lad-diagonal-bifurcation.mp4)

> Just wrapped up a highly complex and rewarding PCI case involving multi-vessel disease with a combination of difficult lesions. The patient presented with a Right Coronary Artery (RCA) Chronic Total Occlusion (CTO) and a critical  Left Anterior Descending (LAD) + Diagonal bifurcation lesion.
> This is precisely where the art and science of interventional cardiology merge! 🛠️
> Procedural Insights:
> RCA CTO: Tackled via a successful anterograde by using 2-Pilot 200 wires and 1x5 mm CTO balloon 
>
> LAD/Diagonal Bifurcation: A true bifurcation lesion that required a dedicated two-stent strategy (Mini-Crush technique) to secure both the main branch and the significant side branch, preventing side branch occlusion.
> Intravascular Imaging (IVI): Meticulous use of IVI (IVUS) was critical throughout the procedure to confirm wire position, optimize stent deployment, and ensure an optimal  result.
> Key Takeaways:
> Complexity is Common: CTOs  in combination  with bifurcations are a significant challenge and require advanced techniques and careful planning.
> Strategy is Everything: A hybrid approach and having a plan B (and C!) are crucial for success in these scenarios.

### Intravascular lithotripsy in dual-territory STEMI

**Posted:** 1 December 2024 · [Original Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/mohd.m.sohil/videos/496859262743566/)  
The video from this post is not copied here because patient details were visible on the cath lab monitors in the footage.

> Sharing a complex PCI case of an octogenarian patient presenting with dual territory acute STEMI (massive heart attack), where coronary angiography revealed a totally occluded and severely calcified proximal LAD and subtotal LCx occlusion (blockage of two major coronary arteries supplying the heart). Primary PCI to LAD and PCI to LCx were performed using a 3x10mm cutting balloon for plaque modification. Following high-pressure balloon dilatation post-stenting, proximal LAD stent deployment remained inadequate. This challenge was effectively addressed with intravascular lithotripsy therapy (8 cycles/80 pulses) to the proximal LAD underexpanded stent. Post-IVL therapy, adequate LAD stent expansion was achieved. The procedure was successfully performed via the radial route, and the patient recovered fully, being discharged after 48 hours of hospitalization. Our team of experts in interventional cardiology is equipped to handle all complex and life-saving coronary and cardiac interventions (cardiac emergencies) round-the-clock (24/7). The key takeaway for the public is to not ignore heart attack symptoms and to seek immediate attention at a cardiac emergency department equipped with 24/7 primary PCI capabilities. Primary PCI remains the most efficient and safest procedure for treating heart attacks.
